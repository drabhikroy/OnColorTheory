# Build 027: A calmer app identity

Build 027 preserves the established On Color Theory app icon while reducing the intensity of its saturated colors, bright highlights, and bloom. The icon now sits more comfortably beside the restrained multicolor identity system used throughout the app without losing its color-space relationships or measurement cue.

Home now clips the app identity to a continuous rounded rectangle. The raster master's outer square can no longer appear around the icon on the landing page.

Version: 0.27.0 (27)
