On Color Theory 1.0.0

If you are looking at this file, you have the right archive.

Quick checks, from the folder that contains this file:

  grep fallbackShortVersion Sources/OnColorTheoryApp/App/AppVersionInfo.swift
      Expect 1.0.0.

  ls Branding/
      Nine files. SVG and PNG of the app mark at seven sizes.

  grep "Coming in a later release" Sources/OnColorTheoryApp/App/AboutView.swift
      The About window names the two sections planned for later.

  grep -c "On Color Theory" Sources/OnColorTheoryApp/Features/Home/HomeView.swift
      Expect 1 or more. The app name is the heading on the Home screen.

  grep "Work on a color" Sources/OnColorTheoryApp/Models/ColorModels.swift
      The sidebar is split into two named groups.

you built earlier:
